## Content type mix (Season 1 balance)

- 35% Speech practice / first words  
- 20% Concept (colors, shapes, numbers)  
- 15% Animals & sounds  
- 10% Movement songs  
- 10% Feelings & family  
- 10% Stories + calm  

---

## Presenter rotation rules

- **Mon–pattern speech days:** Lumi solo  
- **Song days:** Lumi + Pip  
- **Feelings Fridays:** Lumi + Ari  
- **Animal adventures:** Lumi + Kiki (± Ari)  
- **Story Sunday energy (any day calm slot):** Nëna Mimoza  
- **Season specials:** full cast ≤ 2× per season (overstimulation risk)
