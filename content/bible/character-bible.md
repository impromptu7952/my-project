# PlayZone Character Bible

**Style lock:** Warm **2D stylized animation** (soft shapes, big friendly eyes, readable silhouettes). Not photoreal. Not horror-cute. Safe for 1–3.  
**Voice lock:** Standard literary Albanian, parentese, slow.  
**Primary co-view assumption:** Parent nearby.

## 1. Lumi — Lead presenter

### Identity
- **Name meaning vibe:** “Lumi” (river) — flowing, gentle, always moving forward softly  
- **Age presentation:** Young adult teacher energy (not a child; toddlers need a secure adult figure)  
- **Gender presentation:** Softly feminine-coded design (like Ms. Rachel), welcoming to all kids  
- **Outfit:** Butter-yellow cardigan, soft teal shirt, rounded sneakers, tiny star pin  
- **Hair:** Soft chestnut bob with one playful curl; never covering eyes  
- **Face:** Large eyes, thick friendly brows, always visible mouth shapes for speech modeling  
- **Signature props:** Magic soft bag; colored balls; felt shapes  

### Pedagogy job
- Models words, pauses, celebrates attempts  
- Runs hello/goodbye rituals  
- Asks interactive questions to camera  

### Voice direction (for TTS / human VO)
- Tempo: ~20–30% slower than adult conversation  
- Pitch: warm mid-high, never shrill  
- Laugh: short and kind, never mockery  
- Catchphrases:
  - «Përshëndetje, miq të vegjël!»
  - «A thua bashkë?»
  - «Ku është…?» + pause
  - «Shumë mirë!» / «Bravo!»
  - «Mirupafshim, deri herën tjetër!»

### Do / Don’t
- **Do:** Hold objects at chest height; point clearly; wait  
- **Don’t:** Yell, rapid topic jumps, sarcasm  

### Visual prompt seed
> Friendly stylized 2D animated young woman teacher named Lumi, soft rounded shapes, big warm brown eyes, chestnut bob hair with one curl, butter-yellow cardigan over teal shirt, standing in a bright pastel playroom, gentle smile, children’s educational show character design, soft lighting, clean silhouette, safe for toddlers ages 1-3

## 2. Pip — Song & attention sidekick

### Identity
- Small round **yellow bird**, orange beak, tiny blue scarf  
- Size: fits in Lumi’s hands  
- Expresses with head tilts and “pip-pip!” peeps (also models simple Albanian: «Po!», «Ja!»)  

### Pedagogy job
- Echo key words  
- Point with wing  
- Alert sound before a new word (gentle chime + peep)  

### Catchphrases
- «Pip-pip!»
- «Ja!» (with wing point)
- Soft hum under songs  

---

## 3. Ari — Soft bear (feelings & courage)

### Identity
- Plush caramel bear, round belly, honey-colored patch on chest  
- Slightly smaller than Lumi when standing  
- Blushes when shy; grows taller posture when brave  

### Pedagogy job
- Name feelings: gëzuar, i trishtuar, i frikësuar, i qetë  
- Co-regulation: deep breath with Lumi  
- Animal friend bridge episodes  

### Catchphrases
- «Unë jam Ari.»
- «Më jep një përqafim?»
- «Tani jam gati!»

---

## 4. Kiki — Kitten (play & animals)

### Identity
- Soft gray kitten with cream belly and pink nose  
- Loves boxes, yarn balls (safe length), peekaboo curtains  

### Pedagogy job
- Animal sounds (`miau`), spatial words (brenda/jashtë, lart/poshtë)  
- Gentle chase / hide games without fear  

### Catchphrases
- «Miau!»
- «Ku jam unë?»
- «Të gjeta!»

---

## 5. Nëna Mimoza — Elder storyteller

### Identity
- Warm grandmother figure, silver-streak soft bun, lavender shawl, reading glasses on chain (optional)  
- Setting: cozy window seat, soft lamp, embroidered cushion (subtle Albanian textile *feeling*, not literal flag)  

### Pedagogy job
- 3-beat storylets  
- Lullabies and calm endings  
- Family words: gjysh, gjyshe, dajë, teze (introduce slowly)  

### Voice
- Lower, honeyed, very slow  
- Songs almost spoken  

### Catchphrases
- «Ejani afër, të tregoj një histori të vogël.»
- «Njëherë e një kohë… por shumë afër nesh.»
- «Natën e mirë, yje të vegjël.»

---

## 8. Consistency production rules

1. Generate **canonical turnaround + expression sheet** per character once.  
2. Every new still/video is **image_edit / reference-seeded** from canon — never pure regen of “same” character.  
3. Wardrobe stays fixed per season (Season 1 outfits above).  
4. Pip scale: head ≈ Lumi’s fist.  
5. No text baked into character art (localization).
