# Pedagogy Playbook — PlayZone Kids (1–3, Albanian)

## Core principle: Serve and return on screen

Every interactive moment is a **serve** (Lumi speaks/shows) and an invitation to **return** (child babbles, points, signs, or parent echoes). We never punish silence; we wait and celebrate attempts.

---

## Language techniques (use in every speech-forward episode)

| Technique | How on screen | Script cue |
|-----------|---------------|------------|
| Parentese | Warm melody, smile visible | Stage note: *parentese* |
| One idea | Max 3–5 words per line for 1–2 band | «Ja topi.» not long sentences |
| Stress target | Slightly louder/slower on target word | **E kuqe** |
| Pause | Freeze smile 4–7s after question | `[PAUSE 5s]` |
| Gesture | Point, open palms, baby sign | `[GESTURE: more]` |
| Expand | If modeling two words for 2–3: «Topi i kuq.» | Age-band gated |
| Celebrate | Specific praise | «E the! Bravo!» / «Provoje!» |

## Hello / goodbye rituals (locked Season 1)

### Hello (~25–35s)
1. Face fill-frame wave  
2. «Përshëndetje, miq të vegjël!»  
3. «Unë jam Lumi.» (+ Pip peep if duo)  
4. «A thua bashkë sot?» + pause  
5. Tease today’s prop  

### Goodbye (~20–30s)
1. Recap 1–3 words with props  
2. «Shumë mirë sot!»  
3. Wave both hands  
4. «Mirupafshim, deri herën tjetër!»  
5. Soft music button  

Same melody family across episodes for recognition.

---

## Episode goals taxonomy

Use 1–3 goals from:

- **Language:** imitate sound, say word, two-word phrase  
- **Joint attention:** follow point, look at named object  
- **Concepts:** color, size (i madh/i vogël), quantity 1–3  
- **Motor:** clap, stomp, touch body part, stretch  
- **Social-emotional:** name feeling, request hug, calm breath  
- **Routine:** sequence morning steps, clean-up song  
- **Music:** keep simple beat, fill-in last word of song  

---

## Song writing rules

- 4-line verses max for 1–2  
- Same chorus every episode in a series when possible  
- Melody range small (child-hummable by parents)  
- Leave one call-and-response hole: «Ham ham…?» → child  

### Sample chorus scaffold (Colors)
```
Ngjyra ime, ngjyra jote,
Shiko këtu, shiko atje,
E kuqe — clap clap —
Shumë mirë!
```

## Co-play tips (always attach to episode meta)

Parents get 1–2 lines, e.g.:

- «Pauzoni videon dhe prisni që fëmija të tregojë me gisht.»  
- «Përsëriteni fjalën në dhomë me të njëjtin objekt.»  
- «Nëse fëmija bën tingull, buzëqeshni dhe përsëritni fjalën e saktë.»  

---

## Safety & brand

- No food choking close-ups of small parts as toys for mouthing age without parent note  
- No water alone / bath without parent framing  
- Diversity of family figures over time (skin tones, family shapes) in background extras  
- Never shame “wrong” answers
