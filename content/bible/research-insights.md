## 2. Ms. Rachel formula (deconstructed)

Speech-language and ECE sources consistently credit these techniques (also used in live parent–child interaction):

1. **Parentese** — slightly higher pitch, exaggerated melody, warm smile-voice  
2. **Slow rate + clear articulation** — one idea per utterance  
3. **Strategic pauses (3–7s)** — space for babble, signs, pointing  
4. **Repetition** — target word 8–20× per short segment without feeling drill-y  
5. **Gestures / baby signs** with words (more, eat, all-done, please, open)  
6. **Direct camera address** — “Can *you* say…?” / “Where is…?”  
7. **Model → wait → celebrate** any attempt (not only correct words)  
8. **Songs as carriers** of 1–3 target words, not long lyric walls  
9. **Visual support** — hold object, point, show same item from multiple angles  
10. **Low cut-rate** relative to “brainrot” kids YouTube — hold shots so toddlers can process  

**Parent coaching line (always):** screens help most when a grown-up co-views, copies the pause, and repeats words offline.

## 3. What to avoid (age 1–3)

- Rapid montage, flashing, constant zoom-punch edits  
- Dense multi-clause speech  
- Fear, conflict, or “jump scare” humor  
- Irony / sarcasm aimed at adults  
- On-screen text as primary instruction (toddlers can’t read; captions are for parents)  
- Ads, autoplay rabbit holes, related-video pressure  
- Gender-locked “only for girls/boys” framing  
- Over-crowded backgrounds that bury the target object

## 5. Albanian-specific design choices

- **Standard literary Albanian** for all scripts (product lock).  
- **Gender of adjectives:** toddler UI may simplify (`e kuqe`, `e kaltër`…) — scripts can model natural agreement with props (`topi i kuq`, `lulet e verdha`).  
- **Everyday words first:** mama, baba, po, jo, ujë, bukë, më, hap, mbyll, erdha, shkojmë.  
- **Cultural warmth without nationalism overload:** mirëmëngjesi, faleminderit, ju lutem; soft folk-melody *inspiration* (not copyrighted folk lyrics); elder character for lullabies.  
- **Diaspora-friendly:** slow speech helps bilingual homes; optional parent EN summaries in catalog, dialogue always SQ.  
- **Name sounds:** short open syllables for characters (Lumi, Pip, Ari, Kiki) — easy to imitate.

## 6. Episode architecture (default template)

```
0:00  Hello ritual (same 20–40s every time — security through predictability)
0:40  Today’s word(s) tease (show props)
1:00  Teach block A — word + gesture + 5s pause
2:00  Song or movement (same melody family across series when possible)
3:00  Interactive “Ku është…?” / “A thua…?” with hold
4:00  Teach block B or recap A
5:00  Celebrate + goodbye ritual (same 20–30s)
```

**Rituals are brand:** same hello/goodbye reduce cognitive load and increase “I know what happens next” joy.

## 8. Quality bar (production gates)

A package ships only if:

- [ ] Learning goals ≤ 3  
- [ ] Target vocabulary ≤ 6 new words  
- [ ] Pause after every interactive prompt ≥ 4s in edit  
- [ ] No startling audio peaks  
- [ ] Character face readable at phone scale  
- [ ] Captions SQ accurate for parents  
- [ ] Co-play tip written for grown-ups  
- [ ] Linked game or offline activity suggested
