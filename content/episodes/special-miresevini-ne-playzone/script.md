# Script: Mirësevini në PlayZone! (Season special)

**Slug:** `special-miresevini-ne-playzone`  
**Cast:** Lumi, Pip, Ari, Kiki, Nëna Mimoza  
**Age:** 1–3 · **Duration:** ~5 min · **Use:** season open / brand lock  

---

## Goals
1. Meet each cast member by name
2. Wave hello
3. Lock hello ritual for the brand

## Stim note
Full cast — keep cuts slow; one character focus at a time. Max 1 special per month in recommendations.

---

### Open
**LUMI**  
Përshëndetje, miq të vegjël!  
Mirësevini… në **PlayZone**!

`[PAUSE 4s]`

### Meet Lumi
**LUMI**  
Unë jam **Lumi**.  
A thua: Lumi?  
`[PAUSE 5s]`

### Meet Pip

**PIP**  
Pip-pip!

**LUMI**  
A thua: Pip?  
`[PAUSE 5s]`

**LUMI**
